## 2. Core Battery Technology & Thermal Architecture
- **Cell Chemistry**: Lithium Iron Phosphate (**LiFePO4 / LFP**), recognized for intrinsic thermal stability, zero cobalt/nickel dependencies, and non-combustion under puncture testing.
- **Cycle Life & Longevity**:
  - **>6,000 charge-discharge cycles** at 88% to 90% Depth of Discharge (DoD).
  - Designed for a **15 to 20-year operational lifespan** with a standard **10-year manufacturer warranty**.
- **Efficiency & Loss Profile**:
  - Round-trip energy efficiency: **>95% to 98%**.
  - Self-discharge rate: **<2.5% per month** (versus 10-15% in lead-acid).
- **IceBattery® Advanced Thermal Shield (Proprietary Thermal Equilibrium)**:
  - Specially formulated **300µm – 400µm inorganic ceramic coating film**.
  - High solar reflectance in Near-Infrared (NIR) spectrum + ultra-high emissivity (>0.92) in Far-Infrared (FIR) atmospheric window (8–13 µm).
  - Radiates accumulated internal thermal energy directly into deep space.
  - **Reduces internal cabinet operating temperature by 15°C – 30°C**.
  - Sustains full-power operation under extreme ambient heat up to **60°C** without triggering HVAC thermal throttling.
  - Cuts HVAC parasitic cooling energy consumption by **>20%**.
- **Instantaneous Sub-10ms Cutover**:
  - Seamless grid-to-island transfer in **<10 ms (<0.010 seconds)**.
  - Prevents micro-interruptions: Elevators do not stall, Wi-Fi routers do not reboot, and mission-critical IT servers run uninterrupted without separate double-conversion UPS units.
- **Integrated Full-Stack Ecosystem**:
  - **In-House BMS (Battery Management System)**: 3-tier cell-level active balancing, telemetry, over-voltage/under-voltage/over-current/over-temperature multi-stage isolation.
  - **In-House PCS (Power Conversion System)**: Bi-directional high-efficiency inverters supporting 4-quadrant grid support and synthetic inertia.
  - **In-House EMS (Energy Management System)**: Intelligent edge controller executing 6 autonomous algorithms (Peak Shaving, Time-of-Day Tariff Arbitrage, Diesel Minimization, Solar Self-Consumption Firming, Critical UPS Backup, Zero-Feed-in Grid Protection).

## 4. Authentic Financial Models & Client Case Studies

### Case Study A: Invertis University (Bareilly, U.P.) — Hybrid Solar + BESS Microgrid
- **Baseline Challenge**: 2,500 kVA Diesel Generator infrastructure consuming massive fuel, generating severe pollution, and subject to NCR/UP pollution bans.
- **Lith-On Solution**: 1,000 kWh (1 MWh) Lith-On BESS coupled with 900 kWp rooftop solar PV.
- **Operational facts (Corporate Deck p.9, added 25 Nov 2026)**: 500 kW backup support for up to 2 hours during outages; powers 700+ hostel rooms, 140 classrooms and 8 offices; direct green energy export to the grid.
- **Financial Return Metrics**:
  - Baseline Annual DG Diesel Expense: **₹4.14 Crore / year**.
  - BESS + Solar Operating Cost: **₹1.88 Crore / year**.
  - Net Annual Cash Savings: **₹2.26 Crore / year**.
  - System Payback Period: **4.29 Years**.
  - 10-Year Cumulative Savings: **₹17.76 Crore**.

### Case Study B: High-Rise Residential Housing Society (500 Flats, Gurugram NCR)
- **Baseline Context**: 14 Towers, 11 Floors, 500 flats. Total Demand Load (at 60% diversity factor): ~2,000 kW (1,500 kW apartments + 440 kW common area loads: 42 lifts, 14 water pumps, lighting, CCTV).
- **DG Liability**: Four 750 kVA DG sets (3,000 kVA total). DG CAPEX: ₹1.80 Cr. Annual Fuel Cost: ₹6.49 Cr (at ₹98.8/L diesel). Annual Maintenance (AMC): ₹3 Lakh. Total Annual DG OPEX: **₹6.52 Crore / year**.
- **Lith-On BESS Solution**: 7,000 kWh (7.0 MWh) capacity, 2,400 kW power rating, 90% DoD, 95% efficiency, providing 3 hours full-demand backup.
- **Financial & Regulatory Transformation**:
  - BESS CAPEX: ₹8.28 Cr (at ₹12,000 / kWh).
  - Monthly OPEX: ₹15 Lakh (charging off low-cost grid tariff at ₹7/kWh).
  - Annual BESS OPEX: **₹1.76 Crore / year** (versus ₹6.52 Cr for DG).
  - **Net Annual Direct Savings: ₹4.76 Crore / year!**
  - Incremental CAPEX (BESS over DG): ₹7.84 Cr.
  - **Simple Payback Period: ~1.6 Years**.
  - **5-Year ROI: ~204% | 10-Year ROI: ~508%**.
  - Full compliance with Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) Graded Response Action Plan (**GRAP Stage II, III, IV bans on diesel generators**).
