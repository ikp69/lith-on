## 1. Authenticity Classification Policy

To safeguard Lith-On's engineering credibility and avoid deceptive representations, all visual assets are strictly categorized under two clear protocols:

| Classification | Definition | Permitted Sources | Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AUTHENTIC MANDATORY** | Real hardware, factory benches, testing facilities, and verified customer sites. | Lith-On Engineering, Noida Plant, Field Commissioning Logs, CAD models. | **Never substitute third-party or generic battery containers.** All cabinet dimensions, vents, branding, and doors must be 100% accurate. |
| **LICENSED ENVIRONMENTAL** | Wide background architecture, campus grounds, hospital facilities, and EV plazas. | Curated Unsplash, Pexels (commercial license verified), high-resolution stock. | Used solely as background environmental layers behind anchored Lith-On BESS hardware. ## 4. Stock Environmental Curation & Licensing Guidelines

When procuring background environmental images:
1. **Unsplash:** Check that the asset falls under the standard free [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) (commercial use permitted without attribution). Avoid Unsplash+ paid assets unless a team subscription is active.
2. **Pexels:** Verify the image falls under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) (free commercial use permitted, no modification of brand logos).
3. **Lighting & Palette:** Choose daylight architectural shots with clear skies or soft neutral lighting. **Avoid dark night shots, heavy neon filters, or rain effects**, as these violate the light-first architectural design system.
4. **Resolution Standards:** Hero backgrounds must be at least **2400 × 1350 pixels**, saved as WebP (80%–85% quality) to guarantee sub-200 KB load times.
