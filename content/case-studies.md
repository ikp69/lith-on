## Case Study 1: Invertis University (Bareilly, U.P.)
### Flagship Clean Energy Campus Microgrid: 1 MWh BESS + 900 kWp Solar PV

- **Customer Profile**: Premier educational institution with smart classrooms, advanced computer centers, engineering laboratories, and on-campus student housing.
- **The Baseline Challenge**:
  - Primary backup relied on massive **2,500 kVA Diesel Generator infrastructure**.
  - Monthly diesel bills averaged over **₹34.5 Lakhs**, totaling **₹4.14 Crore annually**.
  - Generator exhaust pollution and noise disrupted academic lectures.
  - Pollution control board directives imposed strict operating limitations on diesel engines.
- **Lith-On Engineered Solution**:
  - Turnkey EPC installation of a **1,000 kWh (1 MWh) Lith-On BESS** integrated directly with an existing **900 kWp rooftop solar PV array**.
  - Intelligent EMS programmed for automated solar self-consumption, grid peak-shaving, and seamless islanded backup.
- **Financial & Operational Results**:
  - Baseline Annual DG OPEX: **₹4.14 Crore / year**
  - Post-Installation BESS + Solar Operating Cost: **₹1.88 Crore / year**
  - **Net Annual Direct Savings: ₹2.26 Crore / year**
  - **Simple Payback Period: 4.29 Years**
  - **10-Year Cumulative Cash Savings: ₹17.76 Crore**
  - Displaced over 400,000 liters of diesel fuel annually, reducing campus carbon footprint by over 1,000 metric tons of CO2e.

## Case Study 2: High-Rise Residential Housing Society (500 Flats, Gurugram NCR)
### Replacing Diesel Liability with Zero-Emission Sub-10ms Power

- **Customer Profile**: Large gated residential community comprising **14 towers, 11 floors, 500 apartments**, with extensive common infrastructure (42 high-speed elevators, 14 water booster pump sets, security CCTV, and common area lighting).
- **The Baseline Challenge**:
  - Total society demand load (at 60% diversity factor): **~2,000 kW (2.0 MW)**.
  - Society relied on **four 750 kVA DG sets (3,000 kVA total)**.
  - Diesel CAPEX was ₹1.80 Crore.
  - Annual diesel fuel cost was **₹6.49 Crore** (based on 3 hours daily backup at ₹98.8/liter diesel).
  - Annual generator maintenance contract: ₹3.0 Lakhs.
  - **Total Annual DG Operating Expense: ₹6.52 Crore / year**.
  - Every power outage caused a 10–30 second blackout delay, trapping residents in elevators and resetting home electronics.
  - Delhi-NCR **CAQM GRAP Stage II, III, and IV bans** made running diesel generators illegal during high pollution periods, risking total campus shutdown.
- **Lith-On Engineered Solution**:
  - Installation of a centralized **7,000 kWh (7.0 MWh) capacity Lith-On BESS** with a **2,400 kW power rating** (90% DoD, 95% round-trip efficiency), providing 3 full hours of continuous demand-load power.
- **Financial & Operational Results**:
  - Total BESS CAPEX: **₹8.28 Crore** (at ₹12,000 / kWh)
  - Incremental CAPEX over DG sets: **₹7.84 Crore**
  - BESS Monthly Operating Cost: **₹15 Lakhs** (charging off standard low-cost grid tariff at ₹7/kWh)
  - Annual BESS Operating Cost: **₹1.76 Crore / year** (versus ₹6.52 Cr for DG)
  - **Net Annual Cash Savings: ₹4.76 Crore / year!**
  - **Simple Payback Period: ~1.6 Years**
  - **5-Year Return on Investment (ROI): ~204%**
  - **10-Year Return on Investment (ROI): ~508%**
  - **Instantaneous Sub-10ms Cutover**: Zero elevator stoppages, zero Wi-Fi drops, zero router reboots.
  - **100% GRAP Compliant**: Operates completely legally during all air quality emergency alerts with zero tailpipe emissions and whisper-quiet solid-state operation.
  - **50% Smaller Footprint**: Freed up hundreds of square feet of valuable basement/ground space previously consumed by diesel storage tanks and exhaust stacks.

## Case Study 3: High-Power Commercial EV Fast-Charging Hub (Hyundai Model)
### Eliminating Demand Charges and Transformer Upgrades under DHBVN HT Tariffs

- **Customer Profile**: Commercial highway fast-charging plaza hosting four 60 kW DC fast chargers and high-rate chargers.
- **The Baseline Challenge**:
  - Simultaneous EV charging sessions trigger massive, unpredictable 15-minute demand spikes.
  - Under DHBVN HT commercial tariffs, Maximum Demand Charges account for **40% to 60% of the entire electricity bill**. In the modelled station (240 kW peak, 18,000 kWh a month) the demand charge is **₹96,000/month** (240 kVA × ₹400) out of a total bill of **₹2.42 Lakhs/month** (₹29.02 Lakhs/year). *(Corrected 25 Nov 2026 from Hyundai deck slide 9: ₹2.41 Lakhs is the total monthly bill, not the demand charge.)*
  - Local distribution utility required an expensive ₹45+ Lakh transformer enhancement with an 18-month civil delay before permitting additional charging points.
- **Lith-On Engineered Solution**:
  - Deployed an **LT215 / 450 kWh / 150 kW BESS** configured as a dynamic power buffer.
  - The BESS trickle-charges from the existing grid connection during off-peak hours and discharges instantly during EV fast-charging sessions to shave peak demand draw.
- **Financial & Operational Results**:
  - **Model 1 (BESS Standalone Buffer)**:
    - 100% elimination of demand charge penalties.
    - Annual net savings: **₹12.91 Lakhs / year**.
    - System payback period: **~4.0 Years**.
  - **Model 2 (Hybrid 75 kWp Solar + 450 kWh BESS)**:
    - Annual net electricity savings: **₹20.26 Lakhs / year**.
    - System payback period: **3.9 Years**.
    - **10-Year Net Cash Savings: >₹2.0 Crore**.
    - **10-Year Cumulative ROI: 153%**.
    - Avoided ₹45 Lakhs in grid transformer upgrade capital and enabled immediate station commissioning.

## Case Study 4: Strategic Government & Enterprise Deployments

### Indian Army (Srinagar & Jammu & Kashmir Forward Bases)
- **Deployment**: High-reliability ruggedized LFP battery packs and containerized storage operating in extreme alpine climates.
- **Impact**: Replaced continuous diesel generator operation at forward tactical positions; operates reliably across ambient temperatures from -20°C to +45°C with zero starting failure.

### NTPC (National Thermal Power Corporation)
- **Deployment**: Substation grid-tied storage and frequency stabilization units across Lucknow, Shravasti, and J&K.
- **Impact**: Demonstrated compliance with IEC 61850 substation SCADA communication and automated primary frequency regulation.

### RVUNL (Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam Ltd.)
- **Deployment**: High-ambient grid buffering installation in Jaipur, Rajasthan.
- **Impact**: Leveraged IceBattery® ceramic thermal shield to withstand ambient summer heat exceeding 48°C without air conditioning failure or thermal throttling.

### PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC India Headquarters, New Delhi)
- **Deployment**: Ultra-fast sub-10ms UPS battery backup for corporate offices.
- **Impact**: Zero downtime for enterprise server rooms and corporate IT networks during local utility feeder trips.
