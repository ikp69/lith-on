## The Environmental Cost of Diesel Generation

Diesel generators remain one of the most toxic, carbon-intensive methods of electrical backup in urban and industrial centers:
- **Carbon Intensity**: Burning 1 liter of diesel fuel produces approximately **2.68 kg of CO2 equivalent (CO2e)**.
- **Toxic Particulate Matter (PM2.5 / PM10)**: Diesel exhaust contains carcinogenic microscopic particulates that penetrate deep into lung tissue, contributing to severe urban smog and cardiovascular illness.
- **Nitrogen Oxides (NOx) & Sulfur Dioxide (SOx)**: Harmful acid-rain precursors and respiratory irritants emitted directly at ground level in dense residential and commercial areas.
- **Acoustic Pollution**: Industrial diesel generators operate at sound pressure levels between **75 dB and 95 dB**, causing chronic noise pollution in residential complexes and academic institutions.

## 100% CAQM GRAP Compliance
In the National Capital Region (NCR) and major metropolitan areas, the Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) enforces emergency anti-pollution measures known as the Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP). 
- Under **GRAP Stage II, III, and IV**, running diesel generator sets is strictly banned, risking legal prosecution, heavy financial penalties, or complete operational shutdown for residential societies, commercial facilities, and educational campuses.
- **Lith-On BESS operates with zero tailpipe emissions**, ensuring 100% legal, continuous power compliance throughout all GRAP emergency pollution alerts.

## Maximizing Renewable Energy Utilization
Solar and wind energy are inherently intermittent, generating maximum power during the day when grid demand may be low.
- A Lith-On BESS captures midday renewable surplus that would otherwise be curtailed by utilities.
- By shifting stored solar energy to peak evening hours (4:00 PM to 10:00 PM), Lith-On enables facilities to achieve **70% to 90% clean energy self-sufficiency**, drastically reducing dependence on coal-fired baseload electricity.

## Responsible & Circular Lifecycle Management

### 1. 100% Cobalt & Nickel-Free Chemistry
Lith-On exclusively utilizes Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4). Unlike chemistries requiring cobalt (often linked to human rights and environmental concerns in mining) or nickel, LFP uses earth-abundant iron and phosphate, significantly reducing environmental impact during extraction and refining.

### 2. High Recyclability (>95% Materials Recovery)
Lith-On battery packs and enclosures are designed for end-of-life disassembly:
- Heavy-gauge steel and aluminum enclosures are 100% recyclable.
- High-purity copper and nickel-plated busbars are easily reclaimed.
- Through partnerships with government-authorized battery recycling facilities, spent LFP cells are processed via hydrometallurgical recycling, recovering over 95% of lithium carbonate and iron phosphate for remanufacturing into new energy storage cells.

### 3. Second-Life Repurposing
Cells retired after 6,000 cycles still retain ~70% to 75% of their original capacity. Lith-On supports secondary repurposing programs, transitioning retired commercial cells into low-rate rural microgrids, agricultural solar water pumping, or street lighting projects before final chemical recycling.
