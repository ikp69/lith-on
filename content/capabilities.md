## The 6-Step Manufacturing Process
Every battery module and BESS container follows a standardized, documented assembly protocol:

1. **Cell Sorting & Grading**: Every incoming Tier-1 cell is tested for internal resistance (IR), open-circuit voltage (OCV), and capacity consistency using automated testing benches. Cells are categorized within tight matching tolerances to ensure uniform aging and long pack life.
2. **Structural Module Assembly**: Matched cells are assembled into robust, high-impact structural frames utilizing fire-retardant structural polymers and high-strength aluminum side-plates.
3. **Automated Laser Busbar Micro-Welding**: Cell terminals are joined to nickel-plated copper busbars using high-speed laser welding, creating a permanent, low-resistance metallic bond impervious to mechanical vibration and thermal expansion cycles.
4. **BMS & Sensor Harness Integration**: Precision voltage sensing leads, dual-point temperature thermistors, and active balancing wiring harnesses are routed and secured with automotive-grade locking connectors.
5. **IceBattery® Thermodynamic Coating Application**: The proprietary inorganic ceramic shield is precision-applied to the enclosure, establishing high solar reflectance and far-infrared thermal radiation properties.
6. **Full-Load System Burn-In & End-of-Line Validation**: Complete systems are connected to multi-channel charge/discharge cyclers for 100% capacity verification, insulation resistance testing, dielectric withstand validation, and automated safety trips verification.

## Comprehensive In-House Testing Laboratory
To guarantee field reliability under harsh operating conditions, Lith-On's testing laboratory conducts extensive validation procedures:
- **Insulation Resistance & Dielectric Testing**: Validates high-voltage isolation between live electrical circuits and metal chassis ground (up to 3,000V DC).
- **Thermal Chamber Stress Cycling**: Subjecting battery modules to rapid temperature shifts from -20°C to +60°C to verify BMS temperature trip points and thermal dissipation.
- **Vibration & Mechanical Stress Simulation**: Verifying busbar weld integrity and electrical harness retention under transport and earthquake load simulations.
- **Grid-Simulation & Islanding Test Stand**: Validating sub-10ms UPS transfer switching and harmonic distortion compliance (<3% THD) on a live bi-directional AC test bench.
