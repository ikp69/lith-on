## Product Deep Dives

### 1. Magna-C&I Series (260 kWh / 125 kW)
Designed for commercial real estate, manufacturing plants, educational campuses, and retail malls.
- **Form Factor**: Outdoor all-in-one IP55 enclosure with a compact footprint requiring 50% less space than an equivalent diesel generator set.
- **Smart Peak Shaving**: Automatically caps maximum demand spikes, preventing costly utility penalties.
- **Liquid/Air Cooling Options**: Intelligent temperature monitoring at the cell level ensures that cell delta-T remains below 3°C across the entire pack.
- **Turnkey Integration**: Includes internal AC circuit breakers, bi-directional PCS, isolation transformer, and pre-commissioned EMS edge controller.

### 2. Lith-On-50 Compact ESS (48 kWh / 48 kW)
Designed for residential high-rise common areas, retail bank branches, fuel stations, and decentralized telecom infrastructure.
- **Modular Scalability**: Connect multiple 48 kWh cabinets in parallel to scale from 48 kWh up to 288 kWh as demand expands.
- **Instantaneous Islanding**: Detects grid failure in <10ms, eliminating elevator stoppages and preventing smart-home automation dropouts.
- **Whisper-Quiet Operation**: Solid-state power conversion operates at <55 dB, allowing installation directly adjacent to residential lobbies or parking structures.

### 3. LT215 All-in-One Container (215 kWh / 110 kW)
Engineered for EV fast-charging station peak buffering, commercial microgrids, and remote industrial setups.
- **Heavy-Duty Architecture**: 1P240S LFP string configuration with IP67-sealed cell compartments for dust and water resistance.
- **High-Rate Discharge**: Capable of sustained 0.5C to 1C discharge bursts to buffer simultaneous 60 kW to 350 kW DC fast chargers.
- **Rapid Commissioning**: Factory pre-assembled, wired, and tested in Noida; plug-and-play installation on-site within 48 hours.

### 4. Max-20HC-5000 Containerized ESS (5.0 MWh / 1.25 MW)
Utility-grade energy storage housed in a standard 20-ft High-Cube ISO container.
- **Maximum Energy Density**: Delivers 5,000 kWh of usable storage in a standard 20-ft container footprint.
- **Grid Services**: Provides synthetic inertia, primary frequency response, automated voltage regulation, and renewable capacity firming for large solar/wind independent power producers (IPPs).
- **Substation Ready**: Certified for IEC 61850 and IEC 60870-5-104 SCADA protocols for immediate integration with state and national transmission utilities.
