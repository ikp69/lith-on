# Integrated Energy Storage. Engineered for the Real World.

Lith-On Bharat Energy Private Limited is a specialized Indian designer, manufacturer, and integrator of advanced lithium-ion battery modules and turnkey Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS). Originally established in 2022 as Neoclo Energy Private Limited and evolving into Lith-On Bharat Energy, the company brings together more than 40 cumulative years of power electronics and energy storage engineering wisdom.

Headquartered in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, Lith-On operates an advanced **20,000 sq. ft. manufacturing, assembly, and testing facility** with an annual production throughput of **250 MWh** (50 MWh BESS containers and 200 MWh battery packs). The company also maintains an international supply and innovation hub at Foshan Innovation Torchpark in Guangdong, China.

To date, Lith-On technology powers **over 500 MWh of installations globally**, serving customers across India and exporting to more than **25 countries** throughout Asia, Africa, the Middle East, and North America.

## Leadership & Engineering Pedigree
Lith-On's engineering leadership is anchored by alumni from the prestigious Indian Institutes of Technology (IIT) and veteran energy storage specialists. ## Proprietary 6-Step Manufacturing Process
Every Lith-On battery pack and BESS undergoes a rigorous six-step assembly workflow:
1. **Precision Cell Sorting & Grading**: Internal resistance (IR) and open-circuit voltage (OCV) matching using calibrated automated testers.
2. **Structural Module Assembly**: High-grade insulated module framing with integrated thermal barriers.
3. **Automated Laser Micro-Welding**: Ultra-low resistance inter-cell busbar connections ensuring zero vibration loosening.
4. **BMS & Harness Integration**: Multi-point temperature sensing and active balancing harness wiring.
5. **IceBattery® Thermal Shield Application**: Inorganic ceramic coating applied to internal and external enclosure surfaces.
6. **Full-Cycle End-of-Line Validation**: 100% capacity verification, high-voltage insulation dielectric testing, and complete grid-simulation burn-in.
