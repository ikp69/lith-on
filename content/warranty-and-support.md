### Transparent Warranty Terms
Unlike ambiguous third-party warranties that penalize customers with unreasonable exclusions, Lith-On’s warranty explicitly covers:
1. **Capacity Degradation Protection**: Guarantees that the usable energy capacity will remain at or above **70% of initial rated nameplate capacity** for either 10 years from commissioning or 6,000 full cycles (whichever occurs first), when operated within recommended temperature and C-rate parameters.
2. **Workmanship & Structural Integrity**: Full coverage for structural module enclosures, high-conductivity copper busbars, laser-welded terminals, internal cabling, and the IceBattery® thermal shield coating.
3. **Power Electronics & BMS**: Complete warranty repair or replacement for cell-level BMS boards, active balancers, pyrofuses, contactors, and digital sensors.

## 24/7 Cloud SCADA Remote Monitoring & Telemetry

Every commercial and industrial Lith-On BESS connects to our centralized cloud telemetry platform via secure encrypted cellular and industrial Ethernet links.

- **Cell-by-Cell Telemetry**: Real-time logging of individual cell voltages (millivolt precision), dual-point temperature thermistors, string currents, and enclosure humidity.
- **Predictive AI Anomaly Detection**: Cloud algorithms analyze subtle internal resistance and balancing drifts to detect cell anomalies weeks before an operational failure can occur.
- **Automated Alerts & Remote Diagnostics**: In the event of a warning or trip, our Noida engineering operations center receives instant telemetry, allowing remote diagnosis and rapid dispatch of local field service engineers.
- **Customer Transparency Portal**: Building owners and facility managers can monitor state of charge (SoC), energy savings, diesel displaced, and real-time revenue arbitrage through web and mobile dashboards.

## Site Readiness & Installation Support
To ensure smooth commissioning and preserve warranty integrity, Lith-On engineering teams provide complete site preparation guidelines:
1. **Civil & Structural**: Concrete plinth foundation engineering drawings tailored to soil bearing capacity and container weight.
2. **Electrical Integration**: Detailed single-line diagrams (SLDs) for switchgear interconnection, neutral grounding, external isolation transformers, and utility revenue meters.
3. **Ventilation & Clearances**: Minimum clearance requirements for airflow, maintenance access doors, and external cable entry glands.
4. **Safety & Protection**: Recommendations for external earthing pits (<1 Ohm), lightning protection masts, and emergency stop push-buttons.
