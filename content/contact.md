## Request a Technical Consultation & RFQ

To help our engineering team calculate accurate BESS sizing, payback projections, and diesel displacement figures, please share the following project parameters:

1. **Facility Type & Application**:
   - Residential Gated Society / High-Rise Complex
   - Commercial Office Building / IT Tech Park
   - Hospital / Healthcare Critical Facility
   - Educational Campus / University Microgrid
   - Industrial Manufacturing / Food Processing / Steel Plant
   - EV Fast-Charging Plaza
   - Utility-Scale Substation / Solar-Wind Hybrid Farm
2. **Current Electrical Power Profile**:
   - Existing Connected Sanctioned Load (kW or kVA)
   - Existing Diesel Generator Capacity (kVA)
   - Average Daily Power Outage Duration (Hours/Day)
   - Monthly Electricity Consumption (Units / kWh) and Peak Demand Charges
   - Available Roof or Ground Footprint for BESS / Solar Integration
3. **Contact Details**:
   - Authorized Representative Name & Title
   - Organization / Society Name
   - Mobile Phone Number & Official Email Address
   - Facility City & State
