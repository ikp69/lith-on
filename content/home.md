## Core Value Propositions

### 1. Instantaneous Sub-10ms Power Switchover
Advanced Power Conversion Systems (PCS) detect grid anomalies and transfer to battery storage in less than 10 milliseconds (<0.010 s). Elevators never stall, IT servers never reboot, and Wi-Fi networks maintain zero downtime.

### 2. Native Engineering for 60°C Ambient Heat
Standard energy storage suffers thermal throttling and degradation in tropical climates. Lith-On integrates proprietary IceBattery® Inorganic Ceramic Advance Coating (300µm–400µm), reflecting solar radiation and emitting far-infrared heat into deep space. Internal temperatures drop by 15°C to 30°C, lowering HVAC cooling operational costs by more than 20%.

### 3. Replace Costly Diesel Liabilities (100% CAQM GRAP Compliant)
Diesel generators incur high fuel costs (up to ₹6.52 Cr/yr for a 500-flat community), emit toxic PM2.5 emissions, exceed 85 dB noise, and face mandatory bans under Delhi-NCR Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP Stage II, III, and IV). Lith-On BESS replaces mechanical engines with silent, zero-emission digital power offering 1.6 to 4.29-year financial payback.

### 4. Fully Integrated In-House Tech Stack
Unlike domestic assemblers who rely on generic third-party parts, Lith-On manufactures and optimizes its proprietary cell-level Battery Management System (BMS), bi-directional Power Conversion System (PCS), Energy Management System (EMS), and 24/7 cloud SCADA telemetry.
