## Category 1: Battery Technology, LFP Chemistry & Lifespan

### 1. What battery chemistry does Lith-On use, and why?
Lith-On exclusively utilizes Tier-1 Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4 / LFP). LFP is selected for its superior thermal stability, non-combustible olivine crystal structure, zero cobalt/nickel dependencies, and outstanding cycle life. It does not suffer from thermal runaway under standard nail puncture, crushing, or overcharging conditions.

### 2. How long do Lith-On battery systems last?
Lith-On systems deliver **greater than 6,000 full charge-discharge cycles** at 88%–90% Depth of Discharge (DoD) under 25°C standard conditions. In typical commercial or residential applications cycling once per day, this translates to an operational design life of **15 to 20 years** with over 70% residual capacity remaining at end-of-life.

### 3. What is the round-trip energy efficiency of a Lith-On BESS?
Our systems achieve a round-trip AC-to-AC energy efficiency of **>95% to 98%**, depending on operating C-rates and temperature profiles. The self-discharge rate is extremely low, at **less than 2.5% per month** (compared to 10%–15% for traditional lead-acid batteries).

### 4. What is the difference between Depth of Discharge (DoD) and usable capacity?
Depth of Discharge (DoD) indicates the percentage of the battery's total capacity that can be safely discharged. Lith-On batteries support a high **90% DoD**, allowing you to utilize almost the entire nominal battery capacity on every cycle without accelerating cell degradation.

### 5. What makes Lith-On systems different from standard imported battery packs?
Most low-cost assemblers import generic, pre-packaged battery cabinets with third-party software that is not tuned for Indian conditions. Lith-On designs and manufactures its own cell-level BMS, bi-directional PCS, and EMS in our 20,000 sq. ft. Noida facility, coating our enclosures with proprietary IceBattery® ceramic insulation to withstand 60°C ambient heat.

## Category 2: Thermal Safety & Extreme Climate Operation

### 1. How does Lith-On prevent battery fires and overheating?
Safety is built into multiple layers:
1. **LFP Chemistry**: Non-flammable cell structure with thermal runaway threshold above 270°C.
2. **IceBattery® Ceramic Coating**: Reflects >90% of solar radiation and emits far-infrared heat, lowering internal cabinet temperatures by 15°C to 30°C.
3. **Cell-Level BMS**: Continuous millivolt and dual-point temperature monitoring on every individual cell with automated pyrofuse and contactor isolation.
4. **Fire Suppression**: Integrated aerosol or clean-agent fire extinguishing systems with multi-gas detection sensors.

### 2. Can Lith-On BESS operate in extreme 50°C+ summer heat?
Yes. Thanks to native engineering and our proprietary IceBattery® thermal shield, Lith-On enclosures maintain internal thermal equilibrium and are rated for continuous operation in ambient temperatures up to **60°C** without triggering thermal curtailment or HVAC overload.

### 3. What happens if a cell develops an internal fault?
Our 3-tier BMS detects internal impedance deviations, thermal rate-of-rise anomalies, and voltage imbalances instantly. The BMS isolates the affected module via solid-state contactors within milliseconds, sounds local and SCADA cloud alarms, and maintains system operation on redundant strings where configured.

## Category 3: Commercial & Industrial Energy Economics

### 1. How does a BESS reduce commercial electricity bills?
A Lith-On BESS reduces commercial bills primarily through two mechanisms:
1. **Peak Demand Shaving**: Discharges during high-load facility spikes (such as chillers or motors starting up) to keep registered peak demand safely below contracted utility thresholds, eliminating expensive Maximum Demand Charges.
2. **Time-of-Day (ToD) Tariff Arbitrage**: Automatically charges from the grid during low-cost off-peak night hours and discharges during peak daytime/evening tariff windows.

### 2. Can a BESS be integrated with an existing rooftop solar PV plant?
Yes. Compatible with both AC-coupled and DC-coupled architectures, Lith-On BESS stores daytime solar surplus that would otherwise be curtailed or fed back at unfavorable feed-in tariffs, allowing your facility to achieve up to 80%–90% clean energy self-consumption.

### 3. What is the typical financial payback period for an industrial BESS?
Across verified client deployments, commercial and industrial payback typically ranges from **1.6 to 4.29 years**, depending on local utility tariff structures, diesel generator replacement savings, and daily operating hours.

## Category 4: Residential Housing Societies & Environmental Compliance

### 1. What are the key problems with using diesel generators in housing societies?
- **Fuel Costs**: Diesel power costs ₹30 to ₹40 per kWh (consuming over ₹6.5 Crore annually in large 500-flat societies).
- **Startup Delay**: Mechanical generators have a 10–30 second blackout gap that stalls elevators between floors, resets Wi-Fi routers, and interrupts home appliances.
- **Pollution & Noise**: Emits toxic PM2.5 carcinogens and exceeds 85 dB noise levels.
- **Regulatory Bans**: The Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) enforces Graded Response Action Plan (**GRAP Stage II, III, and IV**) directives banning diesel generator operations across Delhi-NCR.

### 2. How does Lith-On BESS solve high-rise residential power cuts?
Lith-On BESS provides **sub-10ms instantaneous switchover**, meaning elevator motors never lose drive power, corridor lights stay on, and residents experience zero interruption. It operates completely silently (<55 dB) with zero emissions, ensuring **100% legal compliance with GRAP regulations** year-round.

### 3. How much space does a residential BESS require compared to a DG set?
Lith-On BESS requires **up to 50% less physical footprint** than an equivalent diesel generator set and its mandatory bulk diesel storage tanks and exhaust chimney piping.
