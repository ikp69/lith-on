# Our In-House Capabilities

Lith-On's capabilities extend across the energy storage system
lifecycle, from battery pack production to system integration and
quality assurance.

## Battery Pack Production

Battery pack assembly with quality control and a focus on performance
and durability.

## System Design and Integration

Configuring battery systems with intelligent Battery Management Systems
(BMS), Energy Management Systems (EMS), Power Conversion Systems (PCS)
and associated safety components.

## Thermal and Safety Management

Incorporating thermal control, protective systems and fire safety
provisions into integrated BESS designs.

## Testing and Quality Assurance

Assessing system performance, safety and durability against applicable
standards.

## System Configuration and Scalability

Developing configurations suited to different capacities, applications
and installation environments.

## Support Across the System Lifecycle

Installation, technical assistance and ongoing maintenance support can
help customers maintain system performance throughout its operational
life. Service scope depends on the selected product and agreed
arrangement.
