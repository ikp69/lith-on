# Warranty, Installation and Support

## Product Warranty

Warranty coverage depends on the specific Lith-On product and its terms
of use. Before purchase, contact Lith-On to confirm:

-   Applicable warranty duration
-   Performance coverage
-   Operating conditions and exclusions
-   Service and claim procedures
-   Documentation required for a warranty enquiry

Do not rely on a general website description as a substitute for the
warranty terms supplied for the selected product.

## Installation Planning

Installation time depends on system size, site readiness and electrical
integration requirements. A site assessment helps establish a realistic
timeline for installation and commissioning.

Site requirements may include suitable equipment space, ventilation,
electrical connections, safety clearances and, for larger installations,
civil works and additional fire safety provisions.

## Technical Support and After-Sales Service

For product-specific technical support, service availability and
after-sales terms, contact Lith-On directly. Provide the product or
system details, application, operating conditions and a description of
the issue so the team can direct the enquiry appropriately.

## Before Contacting Us

It is helpful to share: - Application and installation location -
Required power and energy capacity - Connected load and backup
duration - Charging source, such as grid or solar - Existing inverter or
power conversion equipment, where relevant - Any known voltage or
communication requirements
