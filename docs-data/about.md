# About Lith-On

## Integrated Energy Storage. Built for Real-World Needs.

Founded in 2023, Lith-On specialises in lithium-ion Energy Storage
Solutions (ESS) and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), backed by
over 20 years of combined industry experience.

We develop and assemble energy storage systems for residential,
commercial, industrial and utility-scale applications. Our capabilities
span battery pack production, system design and integration, and testing
and quality assurance.

From lithium battery modules to integrated and containerised BESS, we
deliver scalable solutions designed to improve power reliability,
optimise energy usage and support renewable energy integration.

Our approach combines engineering expertise, intelligent energy
management and a strong focus on safety and quality to meet the changing
energy requirements of businesses and communities.

## Our Vision

### Enabling a More Reliable and Sustainable Energy Future

To advance energy storage technology and contribute to a future where
reliable, efficient and cleaner power is accessible across industries,
businesses and communities.

## Our Mission

### Delivering Smarter Energy Storage Solutions

To develop safe, reliable and scalable lithium-ion energy storage
systems that help customers optimise power consumption, strengthen
energy resilience and make greater use of renewable energy.

We aim to achieve this through continuous technological development,
integrated engineering capabilities and a commitment to quality and
long-term performance.

## Our Core Values

### Innovation and Engineering Excellence

We develop and refine energy storage solutions to meet evolving power
requirements through practical engineering and intelligent system
integration.

### Safety and Quality

We prioritise battery protection, thermal management, rigorous testing
and quality control throughout the product development and assembly
process.

### Reliability and Performance

We focus on dependable operation, long service life and consistent
system performance across diverse applications and operating
environments.

### Customer Commitment

We work towards understanding individual energy requirements and
providing solutions suited to each customer's application, capacity and
operational needs.

### Sustainability

We support the transition to cleaner energy through efficient storage
technology, renewable energy integration and responsible product design.

### Continuous Improvement

We strengthen our capabilities through ongoing learning, process
improvement and the adoption of evolving energy storage technologies.
