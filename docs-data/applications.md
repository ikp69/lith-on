# Energy Storage Applications

Energy storage requirements differ by site, operating pattern and power
objective. Lith-On develops and assembles solutions for a range of
applications.

## Commercial and Industrial Energy Storage

Solutions for peak shaving, demand charge management, backup power and
energy cost optimisation across commercial facilities and industrial
operations.

Potential use cases include: - Supporting loads during periods of high
electricity demand - Helping manage demand-related electricity charges -
Providing backup power when the system is designed with the required
functionality - Improving the use of surplus solar electricity

Actual results depend on electricity tariffs, usage patterns, system
sizing, efficiency and site configuration.

## Renewable Energy Integration

Storage systems can help capture and utilise renewable energy, support
solar power applications and improve the availability of stored
electricity when required.

Integration depends on the generation source, inverter and power
conversion equipment, control strategy and electrical configuration.

## Microgrids and Off-Grid Systems

Energy storage for localised power networks that operate alongside or
independently of the main grid, supporting remote locations and critical
infrastructure.

## Utility-Scale Energy Storage

Scalable systems designed to address larger energy storage requirements,
support grid operations and improve energy system flexibility.

## Telecom and Backup Power

Lithium-ion batteries can store electricity to support
telecommunications equipment when the primary power supply is
unavailable. The appropriate configuration depends on load, required
backup duration, system voltage and charging infrastructure.
