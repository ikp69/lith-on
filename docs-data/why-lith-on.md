# Why Lith-On

## Designed Around Performance. Delivered with Purpose.

Lith-On combines energy storage products with system design and
integration capabilities to address differing power and operating
requirements.

## Integrated In-House Capabilities

Battery pack production, BESS assembly, system integration and testing
capabilities support greater control over quality and consistency.

## LiFePO₄ Battery Technology

Our focus on lithium iron phosphate technology supports energy storage
solutions designed for long cycle life, thermal stability and reliable
performance.

## Intelligent Energy Management

Battery and energy management systems help monitor operating conditions
and manage stored energy for different application requirements.

## Safety-Focused System Design

Battery protection, thermal management, system monitoring and fire
safety provisions form important parts of our integrated storage
solutions.

## Flexible and Scalable Configurations

From compact energy storage systems to larger containerised and
multi-MWh applications, our solutions can be configured to suit varying
capacity and operational needs.

## Performance in Demanding Environments

Our containerised and customised systems are designed with attention to
environmental conditions, installation requirements and long-term
operation.

## Energy Efficiency and Cost Optimisation

Our solutions support peak shaving, load management, backup power and
improved energy utilisation, helping customers manage energy
requirements more effectively.

## Support Beyond the Product

Installation, technical assistance and ongoing maintenance support help
customers maintain system performance throughout its operational life,
according to the selected service arrangement.
