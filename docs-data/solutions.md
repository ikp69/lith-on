# Energy Storage Solutions

## Energy Storage Solutions Across Applications

Lith-On provides lithium-ion energy storage products and integrated
systems designed to improve power availability, manage energy demand and
support efficient energy utilisation.

## Lithium Battery Modules

High-performance lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO₄) battery modules
designed for reliable operation, long cycle life, modular configurations
and integration into energy storage applications.

## Integrated Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)

Complete energy storage systems incorporating battery management, energy
management, power conversion, thermal management and safety components
to support efficient energy storage and delivery.

## All-in-One Energy Storage Systems

Integrated configurations that combine essential system components
within a compact design, simplifying installation and supporting
streamlined deployment.

## Containerised and Customised BESS

Modular, weather-resistant systems configurable for commercial,
industrial and utility-scale requirements. These systems support
flexible capacity planning and installation in demanding environments.

## Residential Energy Storage

Battery storage solutions for residential applications, supporting
backup power and more effective use of stored energy.

## Find the Right Configuration

The appropriate system depends on the application, power demand,
required backup duration, charging source, installation conditions and
integration requirements.

configuration.
